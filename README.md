## Origin

The Makefile language support (the `com.jetbrains.lang.makefile` packages) is ported from the
[Makefile plugin](https://github.com/JetBrains/intellij-plugins/tree/master/makefile) of JetBrains intellij-plugins,
originally developed at [kropp/intellij-makefile](https://github.com/kropp/intellij-makefile) and licensed under the
MIT License. See [NOTICE](NOTICE).
